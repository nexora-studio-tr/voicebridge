# Code of conduct

VoiceBridge welcomes contributors with different languages, backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, explain technical disagreements clearly and focus criticism on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation, spam and sharing someone else's private information or recordings are not acceptable. Ask before recording or publishing another person's speech. Never post credentials or private conversations in project discussions.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and protect affected people. For a private conduct report, use an available private contact on the maintainer's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/shadow2028-blip); do not publish sensitive evidence in an issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Türkçe: Farklı dil ve deneyimlerden katkılara saygılı davran. Eleştiriyi kişiye değil çalışmaya yönelt. Taciz, ayrımcılık, tehdit ve izinsiz kişisel bilgi veya konuşma paylaşımı kabul edilmez. Bakımcılar zararlı içeriği kaldırabilir ve katılımı sınırlandırabilir. Hassas bildirimleri herkese açık sorun kaydına yazma.
