# Synthetic diagnostic references

The 30 bilingual scenarios in `corpus.json` were written for VoiceBridge. All names, tasks and events are fictional; no Discord audio, user conversation or third-party benchmark text is included. The original text is covered by the repository's GPL-3.0-or-later license.

See [the English guide](../../docs/QUALITY.md) / [Türkçe rehber](../../docs/QUALITY.tr.md) for the reserved split, scoring, critical-fact rubric and exact generation commands. Categories and splits are fixed before measuring. They must not be edited to remove difficult results from the baseline.

Generated audio remains under ignored `artifacts/`. No benchmark WAV or model weight is published here. Voice/model licensing remains separate; retain the [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Published hashes identify local fixtures, but do not imply that regenerating synthetic speech elsewhere produces identical bytes.
